InboxTracker

A personal Gmail inbox dashboard and cleanup tool.

InboxTracker is a single-user, self-hosted application. It runs entirely on its owner's own computer, tracks which emails in their own Gmail inbox are read, deleted, or marked spam, and shows that history on a local dashboard. It can also identify senders whose mail is consistently deleted unread and create a Gmail filter to route their future messages straight to Trash.

It is not a public product, has no sign-up, and is not distributed to or used by anyone other than its own developer.

See the privacy policy for details on what data it accesses and how.
